# Rebel & Rose

[Open the live demo](https://rebel-and-rose-website-demo.netlify.app/)

Collection cards give visitors a quick look at the boutique’s bags, accessories and gifts. **Private shopping** has its own section for the **Sip & Shop** experience.

## Try it

Browse the collections, read the **Sip & Shop** section and open the enquiry form. With sample details, the form opens a draft in your email app.

This is an independent portfolio concept. To preview it locally, download the repo and open `index.html`.
